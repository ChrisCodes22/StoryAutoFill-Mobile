📝 Story Template Helper

Install the bookmarklet in seconds - works on all devices!

👇 Drag This Button to Your Bookmarks Bar

📝 Story Templates

Desktop: Drag the button above to your bookmarks bar
Mobile: Tap and hold, then add to bookmarks

1
Save the Bookmark
Drag the blue "📝 Story Templates" button to your bookmarks bar. On mobile, tap and hold the button, then select "Add to Bookmarks"
2
Go to NetSuite
Open NetSuite and navigate to any page where you enter story descriptions
3
Click the Bookmark
Tap the "📝 Story Templates" bookmark you just saved. A panel will appear with all available templates
4
Search & Select
Search for your template (e.g., "air leak"), click on it, and it automatically inserts into the text field!

✅ That's it - you're ready to go!

Use the bookmark anytime you're on NetSuite to quickly insert story templates. New templates are added regularly and will appear automatically.
